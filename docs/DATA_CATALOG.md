### 1.1 可信度分级 Confidence tiers

【中文】可信度与可得性分开：状态一栏说明数据能否拿到，可信度一栏说明模型现在用的数值有多可靠。参数表 `twin/couplings.csv` 与变量表都有 `confidence` 和 `confidence_note` 两栏。模型状态和输出标为 inherit，可信度取其输入中最弱的一档（短板原则）；只看到转引、未打开原文的来源最多算 T3。

English: confidence is separate from availability. Status says whether data can be obtained; confidence says how reliable the value the model uses now is. Both `twin/couplings.csv` and the variable table carry `confidence` and `confidence_note`. Model states and outputs are marked inherit and take the weakest tier of their inputs; sources seen only secondhand are capped at T3.
