Revision 2026-11-10

The climate layer keeps two nodes: background climate (air temperature, land surface temperature and river temperature; the model uses the current records until the complete historical package arrives) and urban base information. Economic loss and the pavement rutting cost pathway are paused. Hospitals, nursing homes and dialysis centres merge into one medical-facility node. Two lines, A and B, end in time to care as the composite measure.

气候层只保留两个节点：背景气候（气温、地表温度、河温；拿到完整历史数据包之前，模型继续用现有记录）和城市基础信息。经济损失和路面车辙成本暂不考虑。医院、养老院、透析中心合并为"医疗设施"。A、B 两条主线都以就医时间为综合评定指标。
