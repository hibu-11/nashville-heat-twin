Revision 2026-11-20

Background climate and urban base information are inputs only: no relation line between them, and every downstream line starts from one or both. Electric load is split into a base load that does not depend on temperature and a cooling load that does; both drive the transformer hot spot. Supply dependency along the grid topology is removed from the model (deferred D13): a failed substation now cuts only the zone it serves. Rutting enters line A (K11): warming deepens ruts, rough roads slow traffic, and the trip to hospital takes longer. Three feedback loops that act back on temperature are marked (↻ F1–F3): air-conditioning waste heat, its loss during outages, and heated water from thermal plants. Deferred items can be hidden across the whole page. A two-parameter air-temperature fragility tested the same day (3–8% difference in time to care) stays as an option.

背景气候和城市基础信息只作输入：两者之间不画关系线，下游每条线都从其中一个或两个出发。用电负荷拆成不随气温的基础负荷和随气温的制冷负荷，两者共同驱动变压器热点温度。沿电网拓扑的供电依赖从模型中移出（暂缓 D13）：变电站失效只影响它自己供电的分区。车辙进入 A 线（K11）：升温使车辙加深，路面变粗糙使车速下降，去医院的路程变长。标出三个会反过来影响温度的循环（↻ F1–F3）：空调排热、停电时排热减少、火电厂温排水。暂缓项可以从整页隐藏。同日测试过的两参数气温脆弱性曲线（就医时间相差 3–8%）保留为可选做法。

Revision 2026-11-17

Medical facilities are now hospitals only; dialysis centres and nursing homes are removed (deferred D10). Hospitals depend on power and on water: a hospital closes when its zone is out and backup fails, or when system water loss outlasts its on-site supply. Line B splits heat illness from care demand, and keeps the outage path for ventilator and other device users. Each run's added time to care is decomposed by cause (heat illness, device users, hospital power, hospital water, congestion), shown in the table under the map.

医疗设施只保留医院，透析中心和养老院移出（暂缓 D10）。医院同时依赖电和水：所在分区停电且备用电源失效，或全系统断水超过院内储水时长时，医院关闭。B 线把"热病症"和"就医需求"分成两个节点，并保留"分区停电 → 呼吸机等设备用户 → 就医需求"。每次运行增加的就医时间按原因分解（热病症、设备用户、医院停电、医院断水、拥堵），见图下的表。

Revision 2026-11-11

The model now covers the whole of Davidson County (702k people, 159 substations, 112 zones, 12 hospitals, 20 dialysis centres) instead of the downtown box, and line B runs as version 1: heat-illness visits calibrated on Tennessee ED data, nursing-home residents and electricity-dependent residents, ending in ED waiting. Time to care = travel (line A) + waiting (line B).

模型范围从市中心扩到 Davidson 全县（70.2 万人、159 座变电站、112 个分区、12 家医院、20 家透析中心）；B 线第一版已运行：热病症就诊按田纳西急诊数据校准，加上养老院住户和依赖电力设备的居民，最终落到急诊等待。就医时间 = 路程（A 线）+ 等待（B 线）。

Revision 2026-11-10

The climate layer keeps two nodes: background climate (air temperature, land surface temperature and river temperature; the model uses the current records until the complete historical package arrives) and urban base information. Economic loss and the pavement rutting cost pathway are paused. Hospitals, nursing homes and dialysis centres merge into one medical-facility node. Two lines, A and B, end in time to care per ED visit (added travel plus added waiting on the peak day).

气候层只保留两个节点：背景气候（气温、地表温度、河温；拿到完整历史数据包之前，模型继续用现有记录）和城市基础信息。经济损失和路面车辙成本暂不考虑。医院、养老院、透析中心合并为"医疗设施"。A、B 两条主线都以就医时间为综合评定指标。
